# Photography vs AI — honest division of labour

AI renders in **Sketch → Photoreal** are for **visualization and catalog fill**, not a full replacement for a real shoot on a quiet-luxury ethnic-wear brand.

## Use the AI tool for

- Concept visualization before cutting fabric
- Catalog fill and colourway previews
- Trying a design on house models quickly
- Showing what a design *could* look like in a lookbook pose

## Use a real camera for

1. **Fabric hero shots** — AI cannot truthfully render how *your* silk crepe or lawn falls.
2. **Motion / video** — garments are judged in motion (e.g. a farshi trailing as she walks).
3. **Detail macros** — finishing (buttons, hem, weave) that justify price; AI macros are not trustworthy.
4. **Fit truth on a real body** — customers trust a real person in a real photo.
5. **Legal / authenticity** — selling a garment shown only as an AI render risks “not as described.” Real photos of the actual product protect you.

## Product rule

Every generated image in this app is labelled **AI visualization**. Do not present AI outputs as photographs of finished stock without a real shoot behind them.

The tool makes the shoot smaller and cheaper — it does not remove it.
